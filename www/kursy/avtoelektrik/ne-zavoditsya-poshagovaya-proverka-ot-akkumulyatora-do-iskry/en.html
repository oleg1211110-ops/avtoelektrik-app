Won’t start: a step-by-step check from the battery to the spark

What a technician does, in order, when a car won’t start: battery and grounds, starter, cranking speed, immobilizer, fuel, injectors, spark and mechanicals — with measurements, typical figures and cases from Oleg’s blog.

The driver lesson “Why the car won’t start” gives four steps you can do by ear and by eye. A technician needs more: every step needs a measurement with a number that says “OK” or “not OK”, and only then comes the next step. Start by asking what happens when the key is turned — silence, a click, slow or brisk cranking, “catches and dies” — and whether the dash lights up. The general method is in the lesson “Troubleshooting method and working with ECUs”; here it is applied to one complaint. The figures below are typical for a 12 V passenger-car system; check them against the manufacturer’s data.

In this lesson:

  • Steps 3–4. Cranking speed, shaft sensors and the immobilizer
