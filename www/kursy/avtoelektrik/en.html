Online auto electrician course: from the multimeter to the CAN bus

This level is for those who want to make auto electrics their profession: measuring with a multimeter and an oscilloscope, reading wiring diagrams, sensors, actuators, digital buses and a fault-finding method. After the course you’ll know what to measure and where, and how to get from the customer’s complaint to the real cause.
