Car computer diagnostics with a scan tool

Step by step: how to work with a scan tool properly — connecting, the diagnostic sequence, codes and freeze frame, live data, fuel trims, actuator tests, adaptations, compression checks and module communication checks. With animated diagrams and practice simulators.

What you’ll learn

  • Choose a scan tool or adapter and connect properly
  • Follow a diagnostic sequence and find the root cause in a pile of codes
  • Read live data and fuel trims, and catch an intermittent fault on a graph
