Car alarm installation: a course for installers

How to install a car alarm properly: how it works and choosing a system, power and ground, door switches, turn signals, central locking, CAN bus, engine immobilization, sensors, installation, programming, handing the car over to the customer, and troubleshooting. With animated diagrams, practice drills, and labs.

What you’ll learn

  • Understand how an alarm works and choose a system for the car
  • Take power and ground correctly, wire in pin switches and turn signals
  • Identify the central locking type with measurements and connect it
  • Find and check the CAN bus and connect a CAN module
  • Install an engine kill and set up shock, tilt and interior motion sensors
  • Route wires neatly, program the system and hand the car over to the customer
