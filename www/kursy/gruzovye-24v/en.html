Truck auto electrician: 24 V, SPN/FMI codes and fault finding

A specialization for those who have already taken the car course and scanner diagnostics. A truck means 24 volts, a battery disconnect switch, its own control units and SPN/FMI codes. Here is what the other courses don’t have: what breaks most often on trucks and how to track it down. Each lesson has two simulators.

What you’ll learn

  • Understand how 24 V truck electrics differ from a car, and where the search starts — the battery master switch
  • Understand the 24 V supply from two batteries and start a diesel in the cold
  • Read truck SPN/FMI codes over the J1939 bus, and Cummins (FC) codes
  • Find a fault along the circuit on three tractors — Chinese, KAMAZ and European
  • Understand the AdBlue (SCR) system and NOx sensors, and read them in live data
  • Run a scan-tool diagnosis and put together a clear report for the customer

Questions

How much does the course cost?

The course is free: all lessons are open on the website and in the app.

Do I need experience?

The method is the same as on a car: follow the circuit with a voltmeter and find where the plus is lost — but at 24 V and with its own codes. Starting from scratch? Take the “Auto electrician course” course first.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, free and without a promo code. Finish all the lessons and the final test unlocks: 20 questions covering all lessons, and you need at least 16 correct answers for the certificate. It is graded on a 12-point scale: the Certificate of Mastery shows your grade and can be verified by its number, and you can retake the test at any time (the certificate keeps your best grade). It is not a state-recognised diploma: this is a self-study course.

Does it work offline?

Yes: in the Android app, lessons open without the internet.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Lessons open straight away, and your progress is stored on your phone.
