Do your own IP bookkeeping: Form 910, e-invoices, payroll

How to keep the books for your own IP on the simplified regime: what is required, a calendar of deadlines, income, the taxpayer account, payments, Form 910, e-invoices (ESF), employees and Form 200, record keeping and common mistakes. All lessons are open for free. At the start you’ll still need an accountant’s advice so you don’t make mistakes.

What you’ll learn

  • Know what a sole proprietor on the simplified regime must keep, and never miss a deadline
  • Count income correctly and work in the taxpayer account
  • Fill in and file Form 910.00, pay the tax and correct a mistake
  • Issue and check e-invoices and understand the Virtual Warehouse
  • Calculate an employee’s pay and prepare Form 200.00
  • Keep source documents and reconcile with fleet customers

Questions

How much does the course cost?

The course is free: all lessons are open on the website and in the app.

Do I need experience?

No. The course is for a sole proprietor on the simplified regime who wants to keep their own books: from what’s required to Form 910.00 and Form 200.00.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, free and without a promo code. Finish all the lessons and the final test unlocks: 20 questions covering all lessons, and you need at least 16 correct answers for the certificate. The certificate of completion can be verified by its number. It is not a state-recognised diploma: this is a self-study course.

Does it work offline?

Yes: in the Android app, lessons open without the internet.
