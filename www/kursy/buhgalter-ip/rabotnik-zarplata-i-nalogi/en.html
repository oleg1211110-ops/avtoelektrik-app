Employees: pay and taxes

How to figure a shop employee’s pay every month: withholdings, IPN and deductions, take-home pay, the IP’s payments on top and base limits, the payroll sheet and deadlines; what about vacation pay, sick pay, and civil-law contracts, and why pay “in an envelope” is a risk.

How to hire your first employee and what they cost the IP is covered in the course “IP for a repair shop.” Here’s the monthly calculation through a bookkeeper’s eyes: what to accrue, what to withhold, how much to pay out, what to pay on top, what limits the payments have, and how to pull it all into a payroll sheet. Amounts are for 2026: minimum wage ₸85,000, MCI ₸4,325; rates per the Kaspi Guide and clarifications from the ENPF and GFSS.

The order of the calculation

The monthly calculation always goes in the same order. Salary is accrued under the employment contract. From it you withhold OPV, 10%, and health insurance contributions (VOSMS), 2%, then IPN, 10% of income after deductions. The rest is paid out. Separately, at its own expense, the IP accrues OPVR, 3.5%; social contributions (SO), 5% of salary minus OPV; and health insurance deductions (OOSMS), 3%. There’s no social tax on the simplified regime (KGD clarification).

IPN and deductions

In this lesson’s examples, IPN is figured as under the 2025 rules: OPV, VOSMS, and the basic deduction come off the accrued salary, and 10% is charged on the rest. Whether the new Tax Code kept this order and how large the basic deduction is in 2026, we didn’t verify against the code text — confirm with the KGD. That’s why you set the deduction yourself in the lesson calculator. It doesn’t affect the employee’s cost: the deduction changes only IPN and take-home pay, not the IP’s payments on top.

Per the Kaspi Guide, the IPN rate is 15% on an employee’s annual income above 8,500 MCI (₸36,762,500 in 2026). That’s rare for a shop: on average, more than ₸3,000,000 a month.

Base limits

  • OPVR and SO are figured on at least 1× minimum wage, ₸85,000.
  • SO: on at most 7× minimum wage (₸595,000), so no more than ₸29,750 a month.
  • VOSMS: on at most 20× minimum wage (₸1,700,000), so no more than ₸34,000.
  • OOSMS: on at most 40× minimum wage (₸3,400,000), so no more than ₸102,000.
  • OPV and OPVR: on at most 50× minimum wage (₸4,250,000); OPV tops out at ₸425,000, OPVR at ₸148,750.

Payroll sheet and deadlines

It’s handy to keep a payroll sheet for each month: per employee — accrued, OPV, VOSMS, IPN, take-home, OPVR, SO, OOSMS — and totals for each payment at the bottom. You pay the totals no later than the 25th of the next month, and once a quarter you fill in Form 200.00 (lesson 13). The IP’s payments “for yourself” aren’t on the employee sheet; they’re in Form 910.00. Example: a shop has two mechanics, with salaries of ₸200,000 (born 1990) and ₸150,000 (born 1972: no OPVR is paid for him, per the ENPF). Month totals: accrued ₸350,000, OPV ₸35,000, VOSMS ₸7,000, IPN for each with his own deductions; on top the IP pays OPVR ₸7,000, SO ₸15,750, and OOSMS ₸10,500 — ₸33,250 in all.

Pay “in an envelope” isn’t a saving, it’s a risk. On money paid outside the payroll sheet, no IPN or contributions were withheld and the IP’s payments weren’t charged: an audit can assess them with late-payment interest, and the employee gets no pension contributions on that part. Vacation pay, sick pay, civil-law contracts, single payment

  • Vacation pay, sick pay, and compensation: how to figure them and what to withhold from them in 2026, we didn’t verify in official sources — check with the KGD and the Labor Code before accruing them.
  • A civil-law (GPH) contract with an individual who isn’t an IP: per accounting portals, the IP is the tax agent here, and Form 200.00 is filed even with no employees, only GPH contracts. Confirm the 2026 rates for GPH contracts with the KGD.
  • Single payment: per accounting portals, small businesses can pay one payment on wages instead of separate ones. We didn’t verify the 2026 rate and conditions — confirm with the KGD.
Key points
  • From pay you withhold OPV 10%, VOSMS 2%, and IPN after deductions; on top the IP pays OPVR 3.5%, SO 5% of (salary − OPV), and OOSMS 3%.
  • Confirm the 2026 IPN procedure and basic deduction with the KGD: the deduction changes only IPN and take-home pay, not the employee’s cost.
  • Base limits: SO from 1× to 7× minimum wage, VOSMS up to 20×, OOSMS up to 40×, OPV and OPVR up to 50×.
  • Everything on the payroll sheet is due by the 25th of the next month; pay “in an envelope” risks back assessments and late-payment interest.

Check yourself

The salary is ₸200,000. How much OPV and VOSMS is withheld in total?
  • ₸24,000
  • ₸22,000
  • ₸20,000

Answer: ₸24,000 — OPV 10% is ₸20,000 and VOSMS 2% is ₸4,000, ₸24,000 together. ₸22,000 is the IP’s payments on top: OPVR, SO, and OOSMS.

An employee’s IPN deduction went up. What changes?
  • The employee’s cost to the IP
  • IPN and take-home pay
  • OPV and SO

Answer: IPN and take-home pay — The deduction only shrinks the IPN base: less IPN, more take-home. OPV, VOSMS, and the IP’s payments on top don’t depend on it.

The salary is ₸700,000. How much SO does the IP pay?
  • ₸35,000
  • ₸31,500
  • ₸29,750

Answer: ₸29,750 — 700,000 − 70,000 = ₸630,000, which is over 7× minimum wage, so SO is figured on ₸595,000: 595,000 × 5% = ₸29,750.
