Form 910.00

How to fill out and file Form 910.00 for a half-year step by step: what’s on it, where the income comes from, how the tax is figured, what to check before signing with your EDS, and which type of return to choose.

The form number didn’t change in 2026 — it’s still 910.00. But per accounting portals, new reporting forms were approved by Ministry of Finance Order No. 695 of November 12, 2025, and they’re being updated in the account in stages. We haven’t verified the sections and line numbers of the 2026 form, so what follows is a teaching mockup: check the fields against the form in your account or confirm with the KGD.

What’s on the return

  • IP details and period: IIN, half-year and year, type of return.
  • Half-year income — everything received for your work, with no deduction for expenses, net of refunds (lesson 3).
  • Tax — income × rate. The base rate is 4%, and the local council (maslikhat) can change it; for Almaty, accounting portals cite 3% for 2026 — confirm the rate with your local tax office.
  • Payments for yourself — per pro1c.kz, they’re shown on Form 910.00 (lesson 5).
  • EDS signature and sending.

Filling it out step by step

  1. Pull the half-year’s income from your records: KKM receipts plus transfers from businesses to your account, minus refunds.
  2. Sign in to the ISNA account with your own EDS and open Form 910.00; per accounting portals, you can also file it in E-Salyq Business.
  3. Enter the period — half-year and year — and the type of return: the usual half-year return is called a regular return.
  4. Enter the income, then check the tax and your monthly payments for yourself.
  5. Sign with your EDS and send it no later than the 15th of the second month after the half-year.
  6. Pay the tax by the 25th of the same month.
  7. Check your personal account: assessed and paid match, balance 0 (lesson 4).

Example: the shop’s H2 2026

From July through December 2026 the shop rang up ₸5,860,000 on its register — cash, card, and QR; fleets paid ₸1,240,000 into the account; a customer got a ₸40,000 refund. Income on Form 910.00: 5,860,000 + 1,240,000 − 40,000 = ₸7,060,000. Tax at 3%, the rate accounting portals cite for Almaty, is ₸211,800; at the base 4% rate it would be ₸282,400. Parts and bay rent don’t reduce income.

Before signing, run through a short checklist — that way you catch a mistake before sending, not after.

Which type of return to choose

Regular is the usual half-year return. Amended (additional) is for a mistake or missed income found in a return you already filed: it’s filed with the correct figures (lesson 8). Liquidation is for closing the IP. And if you don’t file on time, the ISNA system files a zero return itself; per the KGD, after that you fix it with an amended return.

Key points
  • Form 910.00 — for every half-year: period and type, income, tax, payments for yourself, EDS signature; check the 2026 form’s lines in your account.
  • Income is everything received in the half-year with no deduction for expenses; tax = income × rate (base 4%; for Almaty, portals cite 3%).
  • For H2 2026: file by February 15, 2027, pay by February 25, 2027.
  • Regular — on time; a mistake in a filed return — amended; closing the IP — liquidation.

Check yourself

The shop’s H2 2026 income is ₸7,060,000 and the rate is 3%. What tax goes on Form 910.00?
  • 211 800 ₸
  • 282 400 ₸
  • 70 600 ₸

Answer: 211 800 ₸ — 7,060,000 × 3% = ₸211,800. ₸282,400 is at the base 4% rate, ₸70,600 at 1%.

You found missed income in a Form 910.00 you already filed. Which return do you file?
  • The regular one again
  • A liquidation return
  • An amended return

Answer: An amended return — A mistake in a filed return is fixed with an amended return carrying the correct figures (details in lesson 8).

On Form 910.00 for H2, a mechanic reported income for the whole of 2026. What’s wrong?
  • Nothing: it’s an annual return
  • H1 income was already on the previous Form 910.00 — it was counted twice
  • Only December’s income should have been reported

Answer: H1 income was already on the previous Form 910.00 — it was counted twice — On the simplified regime the tax period is a half-year: Form 910.00 for H2 carries only July–December income.
