Form 200.00

How a simplified-regime IP with employees prepares the quarterly Form 200.00: who files it, where the amounts come from, how to check them against payments, 2026 deadlines, and common mistakes.

While an IP has no employees, its report is Form 910.00 for the half-year. Once you hire a mechanic or sign a civil-law (GPH) contract with an individual, the quarterly Form 200.00 is added. How to hire an employee and what one costs is in lesson 12. Here it’s about the books: where the Form 200.00 amounts come from and how to check and file them. The form is shown as a teaching mockup — roughly like this; check the 2026 form’s lines in your account.

Who files and when

Form 200.00 is filed if you have employees or civil-law (GPH) contracts with individuals; without them, it isn’t filed (Tax Code Art. 114(4), per pro1c.kz). The deadline is the 15th of the second month after the quarter: May 15, Aug 15, Nov 15, 2026, and Feb 15, 2027 for Q4. Payroll payments come earlier — every month by the 25th. The IP’s payments for themselves don’t go here: per the portals, they go on Form 910.00 (lesson 6).

Where the numbers come from

Form 200.00 doesn’t calculate anything new: it collects for the quarter what was already accrued and paid month by month. The source is each month’s payroll sheet and the receipts. For each employee and month you need (2026 rates per the Kaspi Guide and ENPF and GFSS clarifications; details in lesson 12):

  • IIN, full name, and months worked — from the month of hire to the month of termination;
  • accrued income — salary and other pay per the payroll sheet;
  • withheld: OPV 10%, VOSMS 2%, and IPN per the payroll sheet (check the 2026 basic deduction with the KGD);
  • paid by the IP: OPVR 3.5% (except employees born before 1975), SO 5% of salary minus OPV, OOSMS 3%;
  • under a GPH contract — the contract income and withholdings; check the 2026 GPH rates with the KGD.

Example, Q3 2026. Asan earns ₸200,000 the whole quarter: income ₸600,000, OPV ₸60,000, VOSMS ₸12,000, OPVR ₸21,000, SO ₸27,000, OOSMS ₸18,000. Bolat, born in 1970, was hired September 1 at ₸150,000; he has September only: OPV ₸15,000, VOSMS ₸3,000, SO ₸6,750, OOSMS ₸4,500, OPVR ₸0 — he was born before 1975.

Filling in the mockup step by step

  1. Check three months of payroll sheets against the receipts and your personal account: accrued = paid (lesson 4).
  2. Sign in to the taxpayer account with your own EDS and open Form 200.00 for the quarter; type: regular.
  3. Enter employees by IIN and their income — only for months worked.
  4. Check withholdings and the IP’s payments: they equal what you transferred by the 25th.
  5. For Q4, add the year’s totals (per the portals, appendix 200.05).
  6. Sign with your EDS, send before the deadline, and check the status in your account.

Common mistakes

  • The IP’s own payments were added to Form 200.00 — they belong on 910.00.
  • Income was shown for months before hiring or after termination.
  • SO was calculated on the full salary: 200,000 × 5% = ₸10,000 instead of ₸9,000 on (200,000 − 20,000).
  • OPVR is paid for an employee born before 1975, although per the ENPF it isn’t paid for such employees.
  • The form’s amounts don’t match the payments — for example, because of a wrong KBK or KNP (lesson 5).
  • Someone works under a GPH contract, but Form 200.00 isn’t filed.

Reports not filed on time bring administrative liability; check the fine under the 2026 Administrative Offenses Code with the KGD. Nov 15, 2026 is a Sunday: check the KGD calendar for a shift, or better, file early. Ask your tax office (UGD) how to correct a Form 200.00 already filed.

Key points
  • Form 200.00 is filed if you have employees or GPH contracts with individuals — for every quarter, by the 15th of the second month after it.
  • The form’s amounts come from three months of payroll: income, withholdings (OPV, VOSMS, IPN), and the IP’s payments (OPVR, SO, OOSMS); they must match what was paid by the 25th.
  • Income only for months worked; SO on salary minus OPV; no OPVR for those born before 1975.
  • The IP’s own payments go on 910.00, not 200.00; check the 2026 form’s lines in your account.

Check yourself

An IP has no employees on employment contracts but has a mechanic (an individual) on a GPH contract. Is Form 200.00 required?
  • No, only with employment contracts
  • Yes, for every quarter
  • Only for Q4

Answer: Yes, for every quarter — Form 200.00 is filed both for employees and for GPH contracts with individuals. With neither, it isn’t filed.

By what date is Form 200.00 for Q4 2026 due?
  • By Jan 25, 2027
  • By Dec 31, 2026
  • By Feb 15, 2027

Answer: By Feb 15, 2027 — No later than the 15th of the second month after the quarter: for October–December, by Feb 15, 2027.

A mechanic was hired on September 1, 2026. For which Q3 months does his income go on Form 200.00?
  • September only
  • July, August, and September
  • None: he worked less than a quarter

Answer: September only — Income is shown for the months the person worked, starting with the month of hire. In Q3 that’s September only.
