Source documents for a repair shop

Which documents a repair shop prepares for every job and purchase, what to check on an act and a receipt, and how to file source documents by month.

Every job and every purchase at the shop must be backed by a document. An IP on the simplified regime, even without accounting books, must prepare and keep source documents (Art. 202(4) of the Tax Code, lesson 1). Your Form 910.00 income is calculated from them, and they’re also your protection if the KGD has questions.

What documents a shop has

  • Work order or estimate — what we’ll do, on which vehicle, and for how much; agree on it with the customer before work starts.
  • Fiscal cash register (KKM) receipt — for cash, card and QR payments, for the full amount paid (lesson 3).
  • Contract — with a fleet or company: what work, prices, and how payment works.
  • Certificate of completed work (act) — the work was handed over and accepted; both sides sign it.
  • E-invoice (ESF) — when a company or IP pays by bank transfer without a receipt: since 2026, simplified-regime businesses must issue them too (lesson 10).
  • Supplier’s waybill — for parts and materials; if the supplier issued an ESF, keep that too.

A package for every job

It helps to think in packages. A driver (individual): a work order and a fiscal receipt for the full amount. A fleet: a contract, a work order, an act signed by both sides, an ESF, and now and then a reconciliation statement. A supplier: a waybill for what you bought. Per the KGD, for a buyer on the general regime the date the service is received is the date the act is signed, so date the act by when the work was actually accepted.

What to check on an act and a receipt

  • Number and date — by when the work was actually accepted.
  • Who and to whom: the IP as contractor and the customer, with their IIN or BIN.
  • The work matches the work order, and the amount matches the invoice and ESF.
  • Signatures of both sides.
  • The amount on the fiscal receipt equals what the customer paid: a mismatch violates the cash register (KKM) rules (Art. 284 of the Administrative Offenses Code; check the current wording).

Cash register and storage

The cash register produces documents too: register shifts, KKM reports, and data held by the fiscal data operator (OFD). How to close a shift and which reports to keep — we haven’t verified that against official sources, so ask your fiscal data operator and the KGD. For your records, the key thing is that the half-year’s receipts match the income on Form 910.00 (lesson 3).

Source documents must be kept. We haven’t confirmed the retention period against official sources — check it with the KGD, and until then don’t throw out documents from past years. Filing by month is handy: each month’s folder holds a package for every job and purchase, with scans in the cloud as well.

Key points
  • Source documents are prepared and kept at any income level — even without accounting books.
  • Driver: work order and fiscal receipt; fleet: contract, work order, act, ESF; supplier: waybill.
  • The receipt amount equals the payment; the act matches the work order and is signed by both sides.
  • Check the retention period and cash register reporting rules with the KGD; don’t throw out past years’ documents.

Check yourself

Must an IP on the simplified regime without accounting books prepare and keep source documents?
  • No, since they don’t keep accounting books
  • Yes, at any income level
  • Only if income exceeds 135,000 MCI

Answer: Yes, at any income level — The right to skip accounting books doesn’t cancel source documents: they’re prepared and kept at any income level (Art. 202(4) of the Tax Code).

A driver paid ₸45,000 by card. What’s needed for the payment?
  • A fiscal KKM receipt for ₸45,000
  • An ESF for the driver
  • Just the bank receipt

Answer: A fiscal KKM receipt for ₸45,000 — A card payment is settled with a fiscal receipt for the full amount. A sale with a KKM receipt needs no ESF.

A fleet on the general regime signed the act on Sep 30, 2026 and paid on Oct 5, 2026. Per the KGD, what’s the date the fleet received the service?
  • Oct 5, 2026 — the payment date
  • The date the ESF was issued
  • Sep 30, 2026 — the date the act was signed

Answer: Sep 30, 2026 — the date the act was signed — Per the KGD, for a buyer on the general regime the date the service is received is the date the act of completed work is signed.
