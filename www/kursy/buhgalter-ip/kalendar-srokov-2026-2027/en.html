Deadline calendar for 2026–2027

When a simplified-regime IP pays for themselves and their employees, when to file Form 910.00 and pay its tax, when to file Form 200.00, and what happens if you miss a deadline.

A simplified-regime IP has three rhythms of deadlines: monthly payments, Form 910.00 and its tax every half-year, and Form 200.00 every quarter if you have employees. The dates below follow the KGD tax calendar for 2026. It’s handy to enter them once in your phone calendar with reminders.

The deadlines here are for the simplified regime. Anyone who was on the simplified regime in 2025 and didn’t file the notice of the applied tax regime between January 1 and March 1, 2026 was moved to the general regime (OUR) from January 1, 2026 — with different forms and deadlines. Check your regime in your taxpayer account.

Every month — by the 25th

Payments for yourself — mandatory pension contributions (OPV), employer pension contributions (OPVR), social contributions (SO) and health insurance contributions (VOSMS) — and, if you have employees, personal income tax (IPN) and payroll contributions are paid monthly, no later than the 25th of the following month. For October 2026 — by November 25, for November — by December 25, for December — by January 25, 2027.

According to the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS for yourself is paid every month — even in a month with no income — while OPV, OPVR and SO can be skipped for such a month. Confirm this with your local tax office; we’ll go over the amounts in lesson 5.

Every half-year — Form 910.00

On the simplified regime the tax period is a half-year. Form 910.00 is filed no later than the 15th of the second month after the half-year, and its tax is paid by the 25th of the same month:

  • for H2 2025 — file by February 15, 2026, pay by February 25, 2026;
  • for H1 2026 — file by August 15, 2026, pay by August 25, 2026;
  • for H2 2026 — file by February 15, 2027, pay by February 25, 2027: that’s the next deadline;
  • for H1 2027 — by the same rule, August 15 and August 25, 2027, unless the rules change: check the KGD calendar for 2027.

Every quarter — Form 200.00

Form 200.00 is filed if you have employees or civil-law (GPH) contracts with individuals — every quarter, no later than the 15th of the second month after the quarter: May 15, 2026, August 15, 2026, November 15, 2026 and February 15, 2027 (for Q4 2026).

If you miss a deadline

Miss the Form 910.00 deadline and the ISNA system files a zero return for you; after that, mistakes are fixed with an amended (additional) return (lesson 8). Late payment accrues late-payment interest (penya) for every day (lesson 7), and unfiled reports bring administrative liability. If a deadline falls on a weekend, like August 15 and November 15, 2026, check the KGD calendar for a shift — filing early is safer.

Key points
  • Every month by the 25th — payments for the previous month, for yourself and your employees.
  • Form 910.00 — file by the 15th and pay the tax by the 25th of the second month after the half-year; the next deadlines are February 15 and 25, 2027.
  • Form 200.00 — if you have employees or GPH contracts: once a quarter, by the 15th of the second month after the quarter.
  • Miss the filing deadline and ISNA files a zero Form 910.00; it’s fixed with an amended return.

Check yourself

By what date must you make your own payments for November 2026?
  • By Nov 25, 2026
  • By Dec 25, 2026
  • By Dec 15, 2026

Answer: By Dec 25, 2026 — Payments are due no later than the 25th of the following month: for November — by December 25.

By what date must Form 910.00 for H2 2026 be filed?
  • By Feb 15, 2027
  • By Feb 25, 2027
  • By Dec 31, 2026
  • By Jan 15, 2027

Answer: By Feb 15, 2027 — The return is filed by the 15th of the second month after the half-year — by February 15, 2027 — and the tax is paid by February 25, 2027.

The shop has an employee. By what date must Form 200.00 for Q3 2026 be filed?
  • By Oct 15, 2026
  • By Nov 15, 2026
  • By Nov 25, 2026

Answer: By Nov 15, 2026 — Form 200.00 is filed by the 15th of the second month after the quarter: for July–September — by November 15, 2026.
