Monthly payments for yourself

How much a simplified-regime IP pays for themselves in 2026 — OPV, OPVR, SO, and VOSMS — what amount they’re based on, how to pay without a code mistake, and how these payments end up on Form 910.00.

Besides the tax every half-year, a simplified-regime IP makes four payments for themselves every month: pension contributions OPV and OPVR, social contributions SO, and health insurance contributions VOSMS. The deadline is the 25th of the following month (lesson 2). Rates and amounts below are for 2026, with a minimum wage of ₸85,000.

Four payments and what they’re based on

  • OPV — 10% of declared income. The IP declares the income for the math themselves, from 1 to 50× minimum wage a month, so in 2026 the contribution runs from ₸8,500 to ₸425,000.
  • OPVR — 3.5% of the same declared income (it was 2.5% in 2025): from ₸2,975 to ₸148,750 a month.
  • SO — 5% of declared income minus OPV; the base is at least 1× and at most 7× minimum wage, so from ₸4,250 to ₸29,750.
  • VOSMS — 5% of 1.4× minimum wage (₸119,000), so ₸5,950 a month at any income.

A note on sources: the rates for OPV, OPVR, and VOSMS “for yourself” come from the Kaspi Guide and the accounting portal pro1c.kz, and figuring SO on income minus OPV comes from a State Social Insurance Fund (GFSS) clarification (the Social Code itself doesn’t say so directly). Before paying, check the amounts in your bank app or with your local tax office.

Example. An auto electrician declares ₸200,000 a month. OPV is ₸20,000, OPVR ₸7,000, SO (200,000 − 20,000) × 5% = ₸9,000, VOSMS ₸5,950. That’s ₸41,950 a month in total. At the minimum income of 1× minimum wage it would be ₸21,675.

A month with no income. Per the Kaspi Guide, you can skip OPV, OPVR, and SO for such a month, but VOSMS — ₸5,950 — is paid every month. Confirm this with your local tax office.

How to pay without mistakes

You pay through a bank app — for example, the “Taxes” section in Kaspi Pay (per the Kaspi Guide) — through the taxpayer account, or at a bank. Before paying, check four things:

  • IIN — your own, as in your IP registration.
  • Payment type: the KBK (budget classification code) and KNP (payment purpose code) show what it is — OPV, OPVR, SO, or VOSMS. In the app, check that the right payment is selected; if you enter the codes yourself, check them against your bank’s data or confirm with your local tax office.
  • Period — the month you’re paying for, not the month you pay in: October’s payment made in November is a payment for October.
  • Amount — as calculated for that month.

A wrong KBK or KNP code is a common problem: the money is credited to the wrong payment, and the one you meant stays unpaid. Keep your receipts. Ask your bank and your local tax office how to move a misdirected payment.

How this ends up on Form 910.00

Payments for yourself are shown on Form 910.00 for the half-year — per the accounting portal pro1c.kz (April 15, 2026), on 910.00 and not on 200.00, since Form 200.00 is for employees. Which section and lines to use — check against the form in your account: the 2026 forms are being updated in stages. So keep receipts by month: the six months of payments on the return should match what you actually paid.

Key points
  • For yourself, every month by the 25th: OPV 10% and OPVR 3.5% of declared income, SO 5% of income minus OPV, VOSMS ₸5,950.
  • The 2026 minimum at 1× minimum wage is ₸21,675 a month; per the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS is paid even in a month with no income.
  • In each payment, check the IIN, payment type (KBK and KNP), period, and amount: a wrong code means a debt on the payment you meant to make.
  • Payments for yourself go on 910.00, not 200.00; keep receipts by month.

Check yourself

How much does an IP pay for themselves per month in 2026 with declared income at 1× minimum wage?
  • 8 500 ₸
  • 21 675 ₸
  • 5 950 ₸

Answer: 21 675 ₸ — ₸8,500 (OPV) + ₸2,975 (OPVR) + ₸4,250 (SO) + ₸5,950 (VOSMS) = ₸21,675.

What amount is SO for yourself based on?
  • Declared income minus OPV, within 1–7× minimum wage
  • 1.4× minimum wage, like VOSMS
  • Income on Form 910.00 for the half-year

Answer: Declared income minus OPV, within 1–7× minimum wage — Per the GFSS, SO is 5% of income minus OPV: at ₸200,000 that’s (200,000 − 20,000) × 5% = ₸9,000.

A mechanic paid ₸8,500 of OPV but picked VOSMS as the payment type. What happens?
  • Nothing: the amount is the same
  • The bank will fix the code itself
  • The money is credited as VOSMS, and the OPV stays unpaid

Answer: The money is credited as VOSMS, and the OPV stays unpaid — The payment type (KBK and KNP) decides where the money goes. How to move a misdirected payment is something to ask your bank and local tax office.
