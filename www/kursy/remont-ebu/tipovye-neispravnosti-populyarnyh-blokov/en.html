Common faults in popular ECUs

A “symptom → board section → how to check” map for car and truck engine modules, based on general rules, open data sheets and cases from Oleg’s blog.

The lessons in this course are organized by board section: power and communication, outputs, moisture, components. This one works the other way: from the complaint an engine module comes in with, to the section most often at fault and how to check it. The map is built from general repair rules, open data sheets for the parts and the cases Oleg has written up in his blog: Kia and Hyundai, Toyota, Nissan, Honda, DAF trucks. We don’t copy repair manuals for specific modules or vehicle pin numbers — pinouts come only from trusted sources. The blog has no module repair write-ups for Chinese brands yet, so apply the general rules to them.

In this lesson:

  • No communication, engine won’t start
  • Misfire, no spark, coils burning out
  • Cuts out over bumps, failed after a wash
  • After welding, a jump start or an alarm install

The lesson has a hands-on trainer or lab — you can try what it describes in the app.
