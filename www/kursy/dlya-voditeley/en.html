Car electrics for drivers: understand it and check it yourself

Car electrics in plain language — for anyone who drives. After these lessons you’ll understand what’s going on with your car, be able to check simple things yourself and won’t panic when a warning light comes on.

What you’ll learn

  • Understand how your car’s electrical system works and what the dashboard warning lights mean
  • Check the battery and charging with a multimeter, and jump-start the right way
  • Find a blown fuse and replace it with the right one
  • Work out step by step why the car won’t start, and read OBD-II codes
  • Get the car ready for winter and long trips: diesel starting, the parking heater, a checklist
  • Wire in a dashcam, head unit and lights without damaging the wiring

The course is free: all lessons are open on the website and in the app.

Do I need experience?

No. The lessons are written in plain language for people who drive — no electrical knowledge needed.

Yes, free and without a promo code. Finish all the lessons and the final test unlocks: 20 questions covering all lessons, and you need at least 16 correct answers for the certificate. The certificate of completion can be verified by its number. It is not a state-recognised diploma: this is a self-study course.

Does it work offline?

Yes: in the Android app, lessons open without the internet.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Lessons open straight away, and your progress is stored on your phone.
