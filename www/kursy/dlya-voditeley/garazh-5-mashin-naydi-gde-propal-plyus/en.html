Garage: 5 cars — find where the power went missing

The course wrap-up as a garage: five cars — Corolla, Granta, Solaris, Logan, Rio. Each has its own fault: a fuse, a relay, a connector, a wire, a switch. Pick a car and use a voltmeter to find where the power drops out.

Now all together: a garage of five cars, each with its own trouble. The method is the same — follow the circuit with a voltmeter from the battery to the load and see where the power is still there and where it drops out. That’s the fault.
