Minor relearns after disconnecting the battery

After disconnecting the battery, part of the car “forgets” what it learned: window auto-close, the sunroof, sometimes the steering angle and the soft-close. This is fixed by a simple re-initialize, not a repair.

Many convenience features work by learned end stops: the window rising all the way with anti-pinch protection, the sunroof's soft-close, folding mirrors, sometimes the steering-angle zero. After disconnecting the battery or a flat battery this data is reset.

What stops working

The window no longer goes up “one-touch” or the anti-pinch doesn't work; the sunroof won't reach the edge; the soft-close and mirror folding can be lost; on some cars the ESP light comes on until the steering angle is calibrated (covered in the steering-angle lesson).

How to re-initialize

Usually without a scan tool, by procedure: for a window — lower it fully and hold the switch 1–2 seconds, then raise it fully and hold again; for the sunroof — the same at its end stops. The exact order depends on the make — see the manual.

This isn't a window-regulator fault: if “one-touch” disappeared right after disconnecting the terminal or replacing the battery, do the re-initialize per the manual first, don't replace the motor.
Key points
  • After disconnecting the battery, learned end stops are reset: window and sunroof auto-close, sometimes the steering angle.
  • It's fixed by a re-initialize (hold the switch at the end stops), usually without a scan tool.
  • Don't rush to replace the window motor: first re-initialize per the make's manual.

Check yourself

After a battery swap the window stopped going up “one-touch.” What to do?
  • Replace the window-regulator motor
  • Re-initialize: hold the switch at the end stops per the manual
  • It can't be fixed
  • Replace a fuse

Answer: Re-initialize: hold the switch at the end stops per the manual — The window auto-close works by learned end stops, which were reset when the terminal was disconnected. It needs a re-initialize (hold the switch and run it fully down and up), not a new motor.

Why do the windows, sunroof and sometimes the steering angle go off after disconnecting the battery?
  • They broke from a voltage spike
  • The units forgot the learned end stops — a re-initialize is needed
  • It's a coincidence
  • The new battery went flat

Answer: The units forgot the learned end stops — a re-initialize is needed — These features work by learned data (end stops, the steering-angle zero). Disconnecting the terminal erases it — a re-initialize helps, not a repair.
