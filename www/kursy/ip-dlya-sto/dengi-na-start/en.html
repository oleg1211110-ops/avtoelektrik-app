Money to get started

What state support a new IP in Almaty can get in 2026: a 400 MCI grant for equipment and tools, Bastau Business training, subsidized loans, and Damu Fund guarantees — who qualifies and where to check the terms.

A scan tool, an oscilloscope, good tools: at the start, that’s a lot of money. The state helps new entrepreneurs with grants, free training, subsidized loans, and guarantees. Program terms change, and much of this lesson comes from media reports rather than the text of the rules, so check everything on business.enbek.kz and with the program operators.

The 400 MCI grant in Almaty

According to digitalbusiness.kz (May 13, 2026), new entrepreneurs in Almaty can get a 400 MCI grant, ₸1,730,000 in 2026. It isn’t repaid; it’s spent on equipment and tools. The 2026 plan is 380 grants. The report doesn’t name the regulation, so check the terms on business.enbek.kz or at the House of Social Services.

Who can get it

  • Registered unemployed people, or IPs that have been operating for no more than 3 years.
  • And who are also in a social category: large families, recipients of targeted social assistance (ASP), people with disabilities, kandas (ethnic Kazakh returnees), resettlers, and others.
  • A Bastau Business training certificate is required.
  • You apply on business.enbek.kz; advice is available at the Almaty House of Social Services (85 Dostyk Ave.).

Bastau Business: training and certificate

Bastau Business is free training in entrepreneurship basics from the Atameken National Chamber of Entrepreneurs and the Ministry of Labor: you sign up through business.enbek.kz, study online on Skills.enbek.kz, and at the end take a test and get a certificate. That’s how 2022 publications describe the program. Check on business.enbek.kz or at an employment center how long the training lasts in 2026 and how long the certificate stays valid.

Loans and Damu guarantees

According to digitalbusiness.kz (May 25, 2026), Almaty offers loans at 12.6% a year: Jana Business, up to ₸500M, including for beginners; Isker Aimaq, up to ₸200M with subsidies for up to 3 years; and Orleu 2026. If you lack collateral, a Damu Fund guarantee helps. Social enterprises can get a grant of up to ₸5M, with registry status required. The operators are the Almaty Department of Entrepreneurship and Investment and the Damu Entrepreneurship Development Fund.

A loan isn’t a grant: it’s repaid with interest. Before you borrow, work out which revenue will cover the monthly payment. Get the terms only from the operators: business.enbek.kz, the Almaty Department of Entrepreneurship and Investment, and the Damu Fund.

When planning an equipment purchase, remember: no Bastau Business certificate means no grant, and the grant itself is only for social categories. In the lab, work out how much of your own money you’ll need in each case.

Key points
  • The 400 MCI grant in Almaty (₸1,730,000 in 2026) is non-repayable and goes to equipment and tools (per media reports).
  • Who qualifies: registered unemployed people or IPs under 3 years old in a social category; a Bastau Business certificate is required.
  • Loans at 12.6% (Jana Business, Isker Aimaq, Orleu 2026) and Damu guarantees: for when the grant doesn’t fit or isn’t enough.
  • Terms change: check them on business.enbek.kz and with the program operators.

Answer: 400 MCI: ₸1,730,000 — Per digitalbusiness.kz: 400 MCI, ₸1,730,000 in 2026, for equipment and tools. The grant of up to ₸5M is for social enterprises with registry status.

Which document is required for the 400 MCI grant?
  • A bank statement
  • A Bastau Business training certificate
  • An auto electrician diploma

Answer: A Bastau Business training certificate — The Bastau Business certificate is a mandatory condition for the grant (per digitalbusiness.kz).

An IP has operated for 5 years, has a large family, and holds the certificate. Is he eligible for the 400 MCI grant?
  • Yes, large families always are
  • Yes, since he has the certificate
  • No: the grant is for the unemployed and IPs under 3 years

Answer: No: the grant is for the unemployed and IPs under 3 years — Both conditions are needed: registered unemployed or an IP under 3 years old, plus a social category. A 5-year-old IP doesn’t qualify.
