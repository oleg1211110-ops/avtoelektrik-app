Paperwork with private customers

How to document a repair for a private customer: work order, estimate and approval for extra work, act and receipt, warranty; the customer’s rights when there’s a defect, with 10 days to fix it and a penalty of 1% a day.

A private customer is a driver who pays for the repair themselves. Their repair is documented with a work order and estimate, an act, and a receipt. This paperwork serves both sides: the customer sees what they’re paying for, and you have proof the work was agreed and accepted. These are also your IP’s source documents (details in the “IP bookkeeping” course).

Work order

A work order is the contract for a specific repair: what you’ll do, with what, and for how much. It helps to include:

  • the customer: name and phone;
  • the car: make, license plate, mileage;
  • the customer’s complaint, in their words;
  • work and parts with prices (your parts or the customer’s); the total is the estimate;
  • the deadline and warranty terms;
  • signatures of the customer and the mechanic.

Estimate and extra work

Under Art. 36 of the Law “On Consumer Protection,” an estimate is required if the customer or the contractor insists on one. Extra work beyond the estimate isn’t paid for without the customer’s approval. If it’s clear the estimate will be exceeded, you must warn the customer right away. The law text here and below is from the prg.kz database; check the current wording of the articles on adilet.zan.kz.

Found another fault during the repair? Stop and call the customer before starting that work. Write on the work order what they approved and for how much. Without approval, the customer has the right not to pay for work beyond the estimate.

Act and receipt

When the work is done, the customer accepts it and signs a certificate of completed work (act): what was done and what it costs. The act is an IP source document. Run the payment through the register and give a fiscal receipt (lesson 7). A private customer who paid with a KKM receipt needs no ESF (Art. 208(2) of the Tax Code, per the KGD).

If the customer is unhappy

The customer’s rights when work has a defect are in Art. 35 of the same law. The customer chooses what to demand: fix the defect free of charge, lower the price, redo the work, or reimburse their costs of fixing it.

  • The deadline to fix it is 10 calendar days from the request, unless the contract (work order) sets another deadline (Art. 35(4)).
  • If you’re late, the penalty is 1% of the cost of the work for each day, unless the contract or the law says otherwise (Art. 35(5)). The same applies to delays in starting or finishing the work.
  • If the warranty is under 2 years and the defect turns up after it ends but within 2 years, the customer can still make a claim if they prove the defect arose before the work was accepted (Art. 35(6)).
  • For a defect that’s dangerous to life, a claim can be made within 3 years (Art. 35(3)).

Take the complaint calmly and write down the date of the request (the 10 days run from it), what’s wrong, and what the customer is asking for. Inspect the car with the customer present. Once it’s fixed, document it with an act, like any job. Keep copies of work orders, acts, and receipts: in a dispute, they’re your evidence.

Key points
  • Work order: customer, car, complaint, work and parts with prices, deadline, warranty, signatures.
  • An estimate is required if the customer or you insist on it; work beyond the estimate needs the customer’s approval.
  • Acceptance: an act; payment: a fiscal receipt; a private customer with a KKM receipt needs no ESF.
  • A defect is fixed within 10 calendar days of the request; if late, the penalty is 1% of the cost of the work per day.

Check yourself

The mechanic found another fault and fixed it right away without asking the customer. What does the law say?
  • The customer must pay: the work is done
  • The customer pays half
  • Without approval, the customer may refuse to pay for work beyond the estimate

Answer: Without approval, the customer may refuse to pay for work beyond the estimate — Under Art. 36 of the Law “On Consumer Protection,” extra work beyond the estimate isn’t paid for without the customer’s approval. Call and get approval first.

How soon must a defect in the work be fixed if the work order sets no deadline?
  • Within 10 calendar days of the request
  • 30 days
  • 3 business days

Answer: Within 10 calendar days of the request — Art. 35(4): 10 calendar days from the request, unless the contract sets another deadline.

The work cost ₸50,000, and the defect was fixed 3 days past the deadline. What’s the penalty?
  • ₸50,000
  • 1 500 ₸
  • 500 ₸

Answer: 1 500 ₸ — 1% of ₸50,000 is ₸500 a day; for 3 days, ₸1,500 (Art. 35(5)).
