Changing your regime and your information

What to file when your regime or IP information changes in 2026: the regime notice for the move to the new code; a change of address, type of work, or last name; moving to OUR by choice or because you must; VAT registration — where to file and by when.

Your IP information and tax regime aren’t chosen forever. You moved to a different bay, added a type of work, changed your last name, outgrew the simplified regime, or a big customer on OUR cares about your regime: you notify the tax authority of each such change. Let’s go over what to file, where, and by when.

The regime notice: moving to the 2026 code

Anyone who was on the simplified regime, the retail tax, or the fixed deduction in 2025 and wanted to stay on the simplified regime had to file a notice of the applied tax regime between January 1 and March 1, 2026. Per the KGD, those who filed on time use the simplified regime from Jan. 1, 2026, and those who didn’t were moved to OUR retroactively, also from January 1, 2026. Former patent payers who didn’t file the notice were automatically deregistered as IPs on March 1, 2026, effective January 1. Check your regime in the taxpayer account: your returns and deadlines depend on it.

  • ISNA taxpayer account: Submit documents → Notice of the applied tax regime, signed with your EDS.
  • The E-Salyq Business app.
  • Bank apps, such as Kaspi.
  • On paper — at your local tax office (UGD).

Your IP information changed

If you change your bay address, type of work (OKED, lesson 3), business name, or last name, file a notice of changed IP information: on eLicense.kz, in E-Salyq Business (Menu → Services → Change IP data), or in Kaspi Pay (Taxes → Change IP details and tax regime). Per finance.kz and the Kaspi Guide (2026), it’s free and due within 10 business days of the change. If you change your last name, also reissue your EDS on pki.gov.kz or at a TsON; your IIN stays the same (per public sources).

Moving to OUR by choice

You can move from the simplified regime to OUR voluntarily, for example if fleets on OUR care about deductions (lesson 9) or you want to work with VAT: on the simplified regime you don’t register for VAT (lesson 4). Per clarifications in public sources (mybuh.kz, Apr. 24, 2026), the notice is filed before the 1st of the month you’re switching from. Per the Kaspi Guide, if you change regimes in Kaspi Pay after Mar. 1, 2026, the new regime applies from the 1st of the following month, data in the state database updates within 24 hours, and an IP registered in 2026 can change regimes only after Mar. 1, 2026. Run the numbers first: OUR has different taxes and the Form 220.00 return (lesson 4).

Mandatory switch and VAT

If you exceed the simplified-regime limit of 600,000 MCI a year (₸2,595,000,000 in 2026) or start an activity on the banned list (lesson 3), per clarifications in public sources (mybuh.kz, 2026) you file a notice of moving to OUR within 5 business days. From what date OUR applies when income exceeds the limit hasn’t been officially confirmed, so check with your local tax office. For a banned activity, per accounting websites, OUR applies from the 1st of the month of the violation, and one act under such a code is enough.

On OUR, watch your turnover: past 10,000 MCI a year (₸43,250,000 in 2026), the VAT registration application is due within 5 business days of crossing the threshold (KGD statement). Per mybuh.kz (Nov. 28, 2025), late registration carries a fine of 50 MCI (₸216,250), and turnover during the time you should have been registered but weren’t is charged 15% of that turnover; the KGD has also mentioned the 15%. If you move back from OUR to the simplified regime, that’s grounds to deregister you for VAT: there’s no VAT on the simplified regime (KGD clarification).

Key points
  • The 2026 regime notice was filed from Jan. 1 to Mar. 1, 2026; those who didn’t file were moved to OUR from Jan. 1, 2026, so check your regime in your account.
  • Address, OKED, business name, last name: a notice of changed IP information, free, within 10 business days (per public sources); a new last name means a new EDS.
  • OUR by choice: a notice before the 1st of the switch month; over the limit or a banned activity: 5 business days (per accounting websites).
  • On OUR, past 10,000 MCI of turnover: a VAT application within 5 business days; an LLP is a separate legal entity, not a change to your IP information.

Check yourself

In 2025 an IP was on the simplified regime and didn’t file the regime notice by Mar. 1, 2026. Per the KGD, what is its regime from Jan. 1, 2026?
  • The simplified regime, as before
  • OUR from January 1, 2026
  • Self-employed regime

Answer: OUR from January 1, 2026 — Those who didn’t file were moved to OUR retroactively, from Jan. 1, 2026. Those who filed on time use the simplified regime from the same date.

A mechanic moved to a different bay. Per public sources, what does he file and by when?
  • Nothing: the address changes only when the IP closes
  • A new IP registration notice within a year
  • A notice of changed IP information within 10 business days

Answer: A notice of changed IP information within 10 business days — A change of address is a change in IP information: a notice via eLicense.kz, E-Salyq Business, or Kaspi Pay, free, within 10 business days.

You decided to move from the simplified regime to OUR from December 1. Per clarifications in public sources, when do you file the notice?
  • Before December 1
  • Within 5 business days after December 1
  • By the end of the year

Answer: Before December 1 — For a voluntary switch, the notice goes in before the 1st of the month you’re switching from. Five business days is the deadline for exceeding the limit or a banned activity.
