OKED codes for a repair shop

What OKED is, which code an auto electrician and a repair shop should use, when you need a second code for selling parts, which nearby activities are banned on the simplified regime, and how to add or change a code.

OKED is the General Classifier of Economic Activities: every kind of work has its own code. You put the code in your notice when registering the IP, and it shows whether you may use the simplified regime: the list of activities banned from it (Government Resolution No. 970 of November 14, 2025) is built on OKED codes. So a wrong code is not a mere formality.

How to read a code

Read the code left to right, from general to specific. 45 is the section: trade in vehicles and parts, and their repair. 45.20 is motor vehicle maintenance and repair. The fifth digit is the subclass: 45201 or 45202. The full classifier (OKED NK RK 03-2019) is on stat.gov.kz: Reference Books → Classifiers → OKED.

The code for an auto electrician and a shop

  • 45201: maintenance and repair of motor vehicles, except shops in a roadside zone. The main code for a shop or an auto electrician in town.
  • 45202: the same for service stations in the roadside zone of national, regional, and local roads.
  • Per the classifier’s description, this covers mechanical repair, repair of electrical equipment and electronic fuel injection, body work, washing, painting, glass, and tires. Auto electrical work is “repair of electrical equipment.”

The codes and names here come from OKED reference guides based on stat.gov.kz (version of July 1, 2026). Before registering, check the exact wording on stat.gov.kz.

Selling parts? You need a second code

If you don’t just repair but also sell parts to customers, that’s trade, a separate activity. Add code 45.32, retail sale of auto parts; wholesale is 45.31. Pick the five-digit subclass in the classifier on stat.gov.kz. This doesn’t cost you the simplified regime: Section 45 codes aren’t on list No. 970.

What’s banned near a shop on the simplified regime

  • Retail sale of motor fuel: codes 47301 and 47303.
  • Wholesale of fuel: 46711–46719; wholesale of scrap metal: 46771.
  • Leasing of cars and trucks: 77112 and 77122; manufacturing of motor vehicles: 29101.
  • Motor oil and other lubricants fall under group 47.30 in the classifier, not Section 45. Code 47302 isn’t on list No. 970, but 47301 and 47303 are, so pick the code carefully.

Per clarifications in public sources (mybuh.kz, 2026), a “one-invoice rule” applies: even one contract or completion certificate for a banned activity moves you to the general regime from the 1st of the month of the violation. Before a new service, such as selling fuel, selling scrap wholesale, or leasing cars, check the code against list No. 970.

How to add or change a code

If your kinds of work change, file a notice of changed IP information: on eLicense.kz, in E-Salyq Business (Menu → Services → Change IP data), or in Kaspi Pay (Taxes → Change IP details and tax regime). Per finance.kz and Kaspi Guide (2026), it’s free and due within 10 business days of the change; confirm with your tax office. The Law on Permits and Notifications (in the version checked) has no separate license for repair, diagnostics, auto electrical work, tire service, or car washes; separate permits and notifications apply, for example, to vehicle inspection and to installing and servicing tachographs.

Key points
  • An auto electrician or shop in town: 45201; a station in a roadside zone: 45202.
  • Selling parts? Add code 45.32 (retail) or 45.31 (wholesale); find the five-digit subclass in the classifier on stat.gov.kz.
  • Section 45 is allowed on the simplified regime, but nearby activities are banned: retail motor fuel (47301, 47303), wholesale fuel and scrap metal, and car leasing.
  • A new kind of work means a notice of changed IP information: free, within 10 business days (per public sources).

Check yourself

Which OKED code is the main one for an auto electrician with a garage in town?
  • 45202
  • 45201
  • 95210

Answer: 45201 — 45201 is vehicle maintenance and repair, except shops in a roadside zone. 45202 is for stations along roads, and 95210 is consumer electronics repair.

A shop started selling parts to customers. What should it do about OKED?
  • Nothing: repair and sales share one code
  • Close the IP and open a new one
  • Add code 45.32 and file a notice of changed information

Answer: Add code 45.32 and file a notice of changed information — Selling parts is trade, a separate activity. The code is added with a notice of changed IP information; per public sources, within 10 business days.

Which of these is banned on the simplified regime?
  • Electrical equipment repair (45201)
  • Retail sale of motor fuel (47301, 47303)
  • Retail sale of parts (45.32)

Answer: Retail sale of motor fuel (47301, 47303) — Section 45 codes aren’t on list No. 970. Retail motor fuel (47301, 47303) is on the list, so you can’t do it on the simplified regime.
