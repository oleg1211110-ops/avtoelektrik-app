IP for a repair shop: opening and running a sole proprietorship in Kazakhstan

How an auto electrician opens and runs a sole proprietorship (IP) in Kazakhstan: business form, registration, activity codes (OKED), the 2026 tax regime, social payments for yourself, the cash register and Kaspi, paperwork with customers, employees, tax audits, changing the regime and closing down. Based on official sources, with calculators and practice drills. At the start you’ll still need an accountant’s advice so you don’t make mistakes.

What you’ll learn

  • Choose a business form — IP, LLP or self-employed — and register an IP
  • Pick OKED codes for a repair shop and choose a tax regime
  • Pay social contributions for yourself and learn about state start-up support
  • Take payments through a cash register and Kaspi QR, and do the paperwork with customers and fleets
  • Hire your first employee and get ready for audits
  • Change your regime, suspend or close the IP
