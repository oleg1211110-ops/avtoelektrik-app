Social payments for yourself

Which payments an IP makes for themselves every month in 2026 — OPV, OPVR, SO, and VOSMS — what amount they’re based on, the minimum, the payment deadline, and what to do in a month with no income.

Besides the Form 910.00 tax, an IP pays social payments for themselves every month: pension contributions, social contributions, and health insurance contributions. Even if you work alone in your bay with no employees, they’re still yours. The tax is based on half-year income, while these payments are based on declared income, every month.

Four payments

  • OPV, mandatory pension contributions: 10% of declared income.
  • OPVR, mandatory employer pension contributions that an IP pays for themselves: 3.5% of declared income in 2026 (it was 2.5% in 2025).
  • SO, social contributions: 5% of declared income minus OPV; the base is from 1 to 7 minimum wages.
  • VOSMS, compulsory social health insurance contributions: 5% of 1.4 × the minimum wage, which is ₸5,950 a month at any declared income.

The OPV, OPVR, and VOSMS rates for an IP are per the Kaspi Guide and pro1c.kz (2026). How SO is calculated is per the GFSS (State Social Insurance Fund) clarification of May 14, 2026: the base is income minus OPV. The Social Code doesn’t say this directly, so if in doubt, check with the GFSS or your local tax office.

Declared income: 1 to 50 minimum wages

Declared income is the amount OPV, OPVR, and SO are calculated on. You set it between 1 and 50 minimum wages, which in 2026 is ₸85,000 to ₸4,250,000 a month. That gives each payment a minimum and a maximum: OPV from ₸8,500 to ₸425,000, OPVR from ₸2,975 to ₸148,750, SO from ₸4,250 to ₸29,750 (its base is capped at 7 minimum wages).

The minimum for yourself at 1 minimum wage: 8,500 + 2,975 + 4,250 + 5,950 = ₸21,675 a month, or ₸260,100 a year. At a declared income of ₸200,000: OPV 20,000, OPVR 7,000, SO 5% × (200,000 − 20,000) = 9,000, VOSMS 5,950, for a total of ₸41,950.

Deadline and where to pay

A month’s payments are due no later than the 25th of the following month: for January, by February 25; for March, by April 25 (KGD tax calendar for 2026). You can pay in Kaspi Pay under Taxes, in the taxpayer account, or through a bank (per the Kaspi Guide). On your return, payments for yourself go on Form 910.00, not 200.00: Form 200.00 is for employees (per pro1c.kz).

A month with no income

Per the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS for yourself is paid every month, even in a month with no income, while OPV, OPVR, and SO can be skipped for such a month. For example, three winter months with no jobs means 3 × 5,950 = ₸17,850 in VOSMS. Confirm this with your local tax office.

A common mistake is skipping VOSMS while there are no jobs: the debt builds up unnoticed. Check what you owe on your personal account in the taxpayer account.

Key points
  • Every month an IP pays for themselves: OPV 10%, OPVR 3.5% (2026), SO 5% of income minus OPV, and VOSMS ₸5,950.
  • Declared income ranges from 1 to 50 minimum wages (₸85,000–4,250,000); the minimum for all payments is ₸21,675 a month.
  • Due by the 25th of the following month; payments for yourself go on Form 910.00.
  • A month with no income: per the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS is still paid, while OPV, OPVR, and SO can be skipped.

Check yourself

What’s the minimum an IP pays for themselves per month in 2026 (declared income of 1 minimum wage)?
  • 8 500 ₸
  • 21 675 ₸
  • 5 950 ₸

Answer: 21 675 ₸ — 8,500 (OPV) + 2,975 (OPVR) + 4,250 (SO) + 5,950 (VOSMS) = ₸21,675. ₸8,500 is OPV alone; ₸5,950 is VOSMS alone.

Declared income is ₸300,000. What amount is SO calculated on?
  • ₸300,000
  • ₸85,000 at any income
  • ₸270,000: income minus OPV

Answer: ₸270,000: income minus OPV — Per the GFSS, the SO base is income minus OPV: 300,000 − 30,000 = ₸270,000, so SO = ₸13,500.

An IP had no income in February. Per the Kaspi Guide, what do they pay for themselves for February?
  • Only VOSMS: ₸5,950
  • Nothing
  • All four payments at the minimum

Answer: Only VOSMS: ₸5,950 — Per the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS is paid every month, even with no income; OPV, OPVR, and SO can be skipped for such a month. Confirm with your local tax office.
