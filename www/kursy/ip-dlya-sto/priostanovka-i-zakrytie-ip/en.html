Suspending and closing an IP

How an “abandoned” IP differs from a closed one, what’s known about suspension under the 2026 code, and how to close an IP step by step: past reports, employees, register and account, liquidation Form 910.00, payment, and the termination application. At the end: the course’s final test.

Fewer jobs, taking a salaried job, moving, or opening an LLP — you may no longer need the IP. The main thing to remember: stopping work doesn’t mean closing the IP. While the IP is registered, the obligations remain, and debts and interest keep piling up.

You stopped working, but the IP still exists

  • Form 910.00 is filed for every half-year. If it isn’t filed on time, per the KGD, ISNA files a zero return itself (lesson 13).
  • Per the Kaspi Guide, VOSMS for yourself is paid every month, even with no income: ₸5,950 a month, ₸71,400 a year (lesson 5).
  • Anything unpaid becomes debt: interest accrues every day, and as the debt grows, so do the measures, up to an account freeze (lesson 13).

Suspension: first confirm the 2026 procedure

Before 2026, per public sources, an IP could suspend filing tax reports by application. The suspension procedure under the 2026 Tax Code isn’t confirmed by official sources as of October 26, 2026. Before counting on a pause, check with the KGD: in the ISNA taxpayer account, at the call center, or at your local tax office. Until a suspension is confirmed, keep filing reports and paying as usual.

Closing: the procedure per the KGD

In a press release of Jan. 5, 2026, the KGD describes closing an IP in 2026 like this:

  1. File reports for past periods: the regular returns.
  2. If in 2025 you were on the simplified regime, the retail tax, or the fixed deduction and didn’t file the regime notice, file it first: without the notice, the termination application isn’t accepted.
  3. File the liquidation reports: Form 910.00 on the simplified regime or the OUR return.
  4. File the tax application to terminate business activity in the ISNA taxpayer account, under Submit documents.

Preparation: employees, register, account

Accounting websites (mybuh.kz, 2026) describe what to do before the liquidation reports like this: end employment contracts with employees (Enbek.kz), deregister the cash register (KKM), close the IP accounts, file liquidation Forms 910.00 and 200.00 (if you had staff) for the period up to the termination date, pay taxes and payments, and file the termination application. Deadlines for these steps under the 2026 code aren’t officially confirmed, so check them with your local tax office. A VAT payer is deregistered for VAT when terminating activity (KGD clarification).

Before the application, check your personal tax account: debt, interest, and unfiled returns get in the way of a smooth closing. Per accounting websites, if the conditions are met (no debt, you’re not a VAT payer, low risk, and others), the IP is closed under a simplified procedure without an audit; otherwise a desk audit is carried out. Check the conditions and deadlines with your local tax office.

Key points
  • Stopping work isn’t closing: while the IP exists, you file Form 910.00 and pay VOSMS for yourself (per the Kaspi Guide); otherwise you get system zero returns, debt, and interest.
  • The suspension procedure under the 2026 code isn’t officially confirmed; check with the KGD before you stop filing reports.
  • Closing per the KGD: past reports → the regime notice if never filed → liquidation Form 910.00 → termination application in the ISNA account.
  • Before the liquidation reports: end employment contracts, deregister the register, close accounts; before the application: check your personal tax account.

Check yourself

A mechanic stopped taking jobs but didn’t close the IP. Per the Kaspi Guide, what about VOSMS for himself?
  • Pay every month: ₸5,950
  • Don’t pay, since there’s no income
  • Pay once a half-year along with Form 910.00

Answer: Pay every month: ₸5,950 — VOSMS for yourself is paid every month, even with no income: ₸5,950 a month, ₸71,400 a year. Don’t need the IP? Close it step by step.

Per the KGD, what is the last step in closing an IP?
  • Liquidation 910.00
  • The tax application to terminate business activity
  • The notice of the applied regime

Answer: The tax application to terminate business activity — First past reports and, if needed, the regime notice, then the liquidation reports, and last the termination application in the ISNA account.

In 2025 an IP was on the simplified regime and didn’t file the regime notice. Can it file the termination application right away?
  • Yes, the notice isn’t needed anymore
  • Only on paper at the local tax office
  • No: the regime notice first, or the application won’t be accepted

Answer: No: the regime notice first, or the application won’t be accepted — Per the KGD, former simplified-regime IPs without a regime notice file it first; otherwise the termination application isn’t accepted.
