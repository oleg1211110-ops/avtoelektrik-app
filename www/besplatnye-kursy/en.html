Questions

Are the courses really free?

Yes: lessons marked “free” open right away without payment. Some courses have a paid continuation — buying it is optional.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Lessons open in the app or on the website right away. Your progress is stored on your phone.

Which course should I start with?

Drivers — start with “For drivers”. If you want to work as an auto electrician — the course for future auto electricians: the first 8 lessons are free.

Is there a certificate?

A certificate is issued after the full paid course and the final test. Free lessons can be taken without it.

Who made it

The courses are taught by Oleg Dmitriev, an auto electrician from Almaty with 26 years of experience: ECU repair, immobilisers, diagnostics, car alarms.
