TPMS light on? Tire pressure warning, steady or flashing

Steady — a tire is about 25% low, or the first cold morning took a few psi off all four: check them with a gauge and inflate to the door sticker pressure. Flashing for a minute, then steady — the system itself: a sensor’s battery, wheels without sensors or a new sensor not registered.

1. When does the light come on?

Your four tires

2. What to check yourself

  1. Light on and steady — a tire is low. Check all four with a gauge when the tires are cold (before driving, or after the car has stood for a few hours) and inflate to the pressure on the driver’s door jamb sticker — not the maximum printed on the tire.
  2. The light comes on when a tire is about 25% below the recommended pressure, so a lit light means a tire is well low: look for a nail or a screw in the tread.
  3. First cold morning of fall? Pressure drops about 1 psi for every 10°F — the light often comes on overnight. Inflate all four.
  4. After inflating, drive for a few minutes: on many cars the light goes off by itself. Some have a TPMS reset button or a menu item — see the owner’s manual.
  5. Light flashes for about a minute after starting, then stays on — the system itself has a fault: a sensor with a dead battery, wheels without sensors, or a new sensor not registered. A TPMS tool or a scanner shows which wheel.
  6. No sensors in the wheels (indirect TPMS uses the ABS wheel speed sensors): after inflating, reset it in the menu, or it keeps warning.
Don’t drive far on a low tireIt overheats, wears out fast and the car brakes and steers worse. If a tire is going flat while you drive, slow down gently and pull over.

    Questions

    Why is my tire pressure light on but the tires look fine?

    The light comes on when a tire is about 25% below the recommended pressure — a tire can look normal at that point. Check all four with a gauge when they are cold, and inflate to the pressure on the driver’s door jamb sticker.

    Why is my TPMS light flashing?

    If it flashes for about a minute after you start the car and then stays on, the system itself has a fault: a sensor with a dead battery, wheels without sensors, or a new sensor that wasn’t registered to the car. Until it’s fixed, the system doesn’t watch your tires — check the pressure yourself.

    Why does the tire pressure light come on when it’s cold?

    Tire pressure drops about 1 psi for every 10°F the air cools. On the first cold morning a tire that was borderline drops below the warning level. Inflate all four to the door sticker pressure.

    How do I reset the TPMS light?

    Inflate all four tires to the door sticker pressure and drive for a few minutes — on many cars the light goes off by itself. Some have a TPMS reset button or a menu item; cars without sensors in the wheels (indirect TPMS) must be reset in the menu after inflating. The owner’s manual shows how.

    Can I drive with the TPMS light on?

    A steady light means a low tire: drive gently to the nearest air pump and check all four. A low tire overheats and wears out fast, and the car brakes and steers worse.

    How long do TPMS sensors last?

    The sensor has a sealed battery that usually lasts many years; when it dies, the sensor is replaced and programmed to the car.
