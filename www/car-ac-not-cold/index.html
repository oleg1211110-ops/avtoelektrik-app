    2. What you can check yourself

    1. Check the A/C fuse and relay. Swap the A/C relay with a neighboring one that has the same marking: if the compressor now switches on, replace the relay.
    2. Start the engine, press A/C and listen for the compressor clicking on.
    3. Check whether the cooling fan spins with the A/C on. If it doesn’t: check the fan fuse, swap the fan relay with a neighboring one with the same marking, and look at the connector on the fan motor — has it come loose, is there corrosion?
    4. Inspect the connectors for corrosion and the ground on the body. After body work or front-end repairs, make sure every connector is plugged in and locked.
    5. All of the above is fine but the air is warm — the cause is on the refrigerant side: have an A/C shop check the pressures.

    3. Relay clicking or chattering?

    A healthy relay clicks once when it switches on. Chattering means it isn’t getting enough voltage — a weak battery or a poor contact at the terminals or ground — or its contacts are worn. Start with the battery, terminals and ground, and replace the relay only after that.

    Questions

    Why is my car AC blowing warm air?

    Before recharging the refrigerant, check the electrics: the A/C fuse and relay, whether the compressor clicks on, whether the cooling fan spins, and the connectors and ground. In auto electrician Oleg’s practice the cause is electrical in 40–50 % of cases.

    Should I just recharge the AC?

    Don’t rush. If the compressor never switches on because of a fuse, relay or connector, refrigerant won’t help. Check the electrics first; if they’re fine, have an A/C shop check the pressures.

    How do I know if the AC compressor is working?

    With the engine running, press A/C and listen under the hood: the compressor clutch clicks on. No click — check the A/C fuse, the relay and the compressor connector.

    Can a bad relay stop the AC from working?

    Yes. Swap the A/C relay with a neighboring one that has the same marking on its case. If the compressor now clicks on, replace the relay.

    Why does the radiator fan matter for the AC?

    With the A/C on, the cooling fan should spin. If it doesn’t, check its fuse, swap its relay with a neighboring one with the same marking and look at the connector on the fan motor.
