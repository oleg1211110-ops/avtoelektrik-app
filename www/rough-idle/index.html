Rough idle? Narrow it down before you buy parts

A rough idle is the engine shaking, surging up and down, or nearly stalling when you're stopped — and it's loudest at idle for a simple reason: at idle there's no road noise or load to mask a single weak cylinder or a little extra air. The cause is almost always one of a handful of families, and the mistake most people make is swapping parts from the top of that list and hoping. Two quick tests — the idle-vs-2500 fuel-trim test and the swap-to-neighbor method — tell you which family you're in before you spend a cent.

Quick-read your idleMisfire & the swap method

1. Quick read

Quick read: find your answer fast

Pick what the idle is doing. You get the family it's in — a misfire, an air leak, the MAF/fuel, or a weak cylinder — and the test to confirm it.

2. The families behind a rough idle

Almost every rough idle lands in one of these:

  • A misfire (ignition) — a dead coil, a fouled/worn plug, or a bad plug wire drops a cylinder; the most common cause, and the one the swap method nails (see engine misfire).
  • A vacuum / air leak — unmetered air past the MAF (a cracked intake boot, a split vacuum hose, a leaking intake gasket, a bad PCV) leans the mixture and upsets the idle (see vacuum leak).
  • A dirty MAF or throttle body — a fouled MAF under-reports air and the computer under-fuels; a carboned throttle body and idle passage make the idle hunt or hang (see MAF sensor).
  • Fuel — low fuel pressure (a weak pump or clogged filter) or dirty/leaking injectors.
  • EGR stuck open — dumps exhaust into the intake at idle, so it stumbles and the metered airflow drops.
  • A weak cylinder (mechanical) — low compression from worn rings or a burned/tight valve; no plug, coil or injector cures it.
  • A lying coolant temperature sensor — the wrong warm-up idle, so it runs high, erratic, or won't settle.

The oxygen sensor usually isn't the cause here — it's the messenger that reports the lean or rich condition the family above is making.

3. Test 1 — idle vs 2500 rpm (fuel trim)

The fastest way to separate an air leak from a sensor or fuel problem; you only need a scanner that shows fuel trim. Watch STFT + LTFT at idle, then hold ~2500 rpm. Strongly positive (lean) at idle but near zero at 2500 → a vacuum (air) leak. Lean about the same at both → the MAF is under-reading, or fuel pressure is low. Strongly negative (rich) → the fuel side (high fuel pressure, leaking injectors, a stuck-open EVAP purge, or a coolant temp sensor reading cold). A code sets when long-term trim pushes toward about ±25%; normal sits within roughly ±10%.

4. Test 2 — swap to the neighbor (for a misfire)

If the roughness is a misfire, don't replace parts in a row — move one suspect part to a neighboring cylinder and let the misfire tell you what's wrong. Find the cylinder (P0301–P0312, or the Misfire Current/History counters), then swap the coil with a good neighbor, clear, restart — misfire moves → that coil. Didn't move? Swap the plug — follows → replace it (read the fouling). Still the same? Swap the injector — follows → it's clogged/weak. Nothing moved it? Measure compression — low = a weak cylinder (valves or rings), which is mechanical. Full detail on the engine misfire page.

5. What you can do yourself, step by step

  1. Scan it. Read the codes — misfire (P0300–P0312), MAF (P0100–P0104), lean/rich (P0171/P0172), EGR. No code doesn't mean nothing's wrong; a dirty MAF or small leak often throws none.
  2. Run the idle-vs-2500 trim test to place it — vacuum leak, MAF/fuel, or rich. If it's a misfire, use the swap method to pin the cylinder and the part.
  3. Inspect for a vacuum leak — cracked intake boot, split hoses, PCV, a hiss that changes the idle when you pinch a line. Smoke-testing is the sure way.
  4. Clean the MAF and throttle body if dirty — MAF cleaner only on the element (never carb or brake cleaner). After cleaning the throttle body, do the throttle/idle relearn, or the idle will hunt or stall until the computer re-adapts.
  5. Gauge the fuel pressure if the mixture is lean at both idle and 2500 and the MAF reads plausibly — a weak pump or clogged filter leans it the same way.

6. Common misdiagnoses to avoid

  • "Replace all the plugs and coils" — it works by accident; you happened to replace the one bad part and paid for the whole set.
  • Buying a MAF for a lean idle that's really a vacuum leak — run the idle-vs-2500 test first.
  • Replacing the O2 sensor for a lean/rich code — it's reporting the mixture the fuel system or a leak is making, not causing it.
  • Cleaning the throttle body and skipping the relearn — then chasing the hunting idle you just created.
  • Ignoring a flashing check engine light to "drive it a bit" — a hard misfire at idle still dumps raw fuel into the catalytic converter and melts it.

Questions

Why does my car shake only at idle and run fine at speed?

A single weak or missing cylinder shows worst at idle, when no load or road noise masks it. Read the misfire counters and use the swap method to find the cylinder.

What are the most common causes of a rough idle?

A misfire (coil, plug, injector), a vacuum/air leak, a dirty MAF or throttle body, low fuel pressure or dirty injectors, an EGR stuck open, a weak cylinder, or a coolant temp sensor reading wrong.

How do I tell a vacuum leak from a bad MAF?

Watch fuel trim at idle and at 2500 rpm. Strongly lean at idle but near zero at 2500 is a vacuum leak; lean about the same at both is a MAF under-reading or low fuel pressure.

Why does my idle surge or hunt up and down?

Usually unmetered air (a vacuum leak), a dirty throttle body and idle passage, or a lost idle adaptation. Smoke-test the intake, clean the throttle body and do the throttle relearn.

My idle got worse after I cleaned the throttle body — why?

The computer lost its learned idle values. Do the throttle/idle relearn so it re-adapts, and double-check you didn't leave a hose off or loosen a clamp.

Is a rough idle always the spark plugs?

No. It can be ignition, a leak, the MAF or throttle, fuel, EGR or a mechanically weak cylinder. The idle-vs-2500 test and the swap method tell you which — before you buy anything.
