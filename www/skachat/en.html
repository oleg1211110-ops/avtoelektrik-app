Android phone + Bluetooth adapter

The adapter in the OBD-II port under the dashboard, the phone in your hand: engine, ABS, airbag and transmission codes, live data, a pre-purchase check, a PDF report.

  1. Download and install the app.
  2. Plug in the adapter and turn the ignition on.

Windows computer + Scanmatik

The program connects the app to J2534 adapters: Scanmatik 2 PRO, Scanmatik 3 and others whose driver is installed in Windows. Scanmatik 3 also works over CAN FD; newer BMW, VW, Mercedes, Volvo and Jaguar Land Rover over DoIP with an ENET cable; trucks over J1939.

  1. Download the program and run the file — the app opens in your browser.
  2. Close the Scanmatik software: the adapter works with one program at a time.
  3. Connect the adapter to the car and the computer, turn the ignition on and click “Find adapters”.
16 MB file, Windows 10 and 11. Read and clear codes only: no writing to modules, no coding, no flashing.

For trucks

24 V trucks (J1939)

Trucks are read over J1939: with the Windows program and Scanmatik, or with a Bluetooth adapter in trial mode.

  1. A 12 V adapter (vLinker MC+ and most ELM327s) only through a 24→12 V converter.
  2. A 24 V socket has a split in the middle key: a 12 V plug with a solid key won’t fit into it.
  3. Car-type socket on a 24 V system — don’t plug in directly.
Scanmatik connects to a truck without a converter.

Oleg’s tips on adapters

  • Oleg recommends the vLinker MC+ (Vgate): it supports all OBD-II protocols and doesn’t drain the battery while left in the port.
  • Cheap ELM327 clones are unstable, and in Oleg’s experience they sometimes “kill” the car — after connecting one it stops starting.
  • Check your adapter in the app: Scanner → Adapter check shows what it can do.
  • Plug the adapter in with the ignition off, then turn the ignition on or start the engine.

Questions

Is the scanner free?

Yes. The Android app, the Windows program and the online version are free, with no sign-up. You only need an adapter.

Does it work with Scanmatik 3 and CAN FD?

Yes: the Windows program tries CAN FD on its own after regular CAN, and reads newer cars with DoIP through the Scanmatik 3 network port or an ENET cable. This is a new feature: if a car doesn’t read, send the program log (scanmatik.log) to Oleg on WhatsApp and we’ll sort it out.

Can the scanner damage a module?

No. The app and the program only read data and clear codes with standard commands. The Windows program has an allow list: writing to modules, coding and flashing never reach the car.

Why does Windows show a warning when I run it?

The program is new and Windows doesn’t know it yet. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. Download the program only from this site.

Does it work on iPhone?

On iPhone the online version works: fault codes, tips and the scanner on a virtual car. Connecting to an adapter is in the Android app or on a computer with Scanmatik.
