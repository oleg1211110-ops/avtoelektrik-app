How to change a car battery: step by step, and what to reset after

Changing a car battery takes 15–30 minutes and a 10 mm wrench. The order matters: the negative comes off first and goes back on last. On newer cars with start-stop the new battery must be the same type — and registered with a scan tool.

    After the swap: what went wrong?

    2. Before you buy: is it really the battery?

    A dead battery is often not the battery’s fault: corroded terminals, a weak alternator or something draining it overnight. Check first — test the battery with a multimeter and the alternator. Then pick the new battery:

    • Same group size (for example 24F, 35, 48/H6, 65, 94R/H7) — it fits the tray and the hold-down.
    • The positive (+) on the same side as the old one, or the cables won’t reach.
    • Cold cranking amps (CCA) at least as high as the original.
    • The same type: if the car came with AGM or EFB (usually start-stop), put AGM or EFB back.

    3. How to change a car battery

    1. Park, turn everything off and take the key out. Put on gloves and eye protection. If you don’t want to lose the radio and clock settings, plug a memory saver into the OBD-II port.
    2. Disconnect the negative (−, black) cable first, then the positive (+, red). Usually a 10 mm wrench.
    3. Remove the hold-down — a bar on top or a clamp at the base of the battery.
    4. Lift the old battery straight up and keep it upright: it weighs 30–50 lb (14–23 kg) and has acid inside.
    5. Clean the tray and the cable clamps. White or blue-green crust — clean it with baking soda and water.
    6. Put the new battery in with the positive (+) on the same side as before, and fasten the hold-down so it can’t move.
    7. Connect the positive (+) first, then the negative (−). Tighten each clamp so it doesn’t turn by hand, and coat the terminals with a little dielectric grease.
    8. Start the engine. On cars with start-stop or a battery sensor (BMW, Audi, VW, Mercedes, Volvo and others) register the new battery with a scan tool.
    9. Take the old battery back to the store — most refund the core charge.
    Never connect it backwardsThe + cable on the − post means a big spark, a blown main fuse and sometimes burned electronics. Check the + and − marks on the battery before you tighten anything.

    4. After the swap: what to reset

    Clock and radio presets; the one-touch windows (hold the switch down, then up, a few seconds each way); sometimes a radio code. The engine computer also forgets the idle — let it idle 5–10 minutes. On cars with start-stop, register the battery. In auto electrician Oleg’s experience, an unregistered AGM battery gets overcharged: as the old battery aged, the car raised the charging voltage to make it last — and keeps doing that for the new one.

    Questions

    Which terminal do I disconnect first when changing a car battery?

    The negative (−, black) first, then the positive (+, red). When you install the new battery: positive first, negative last. That way a wrench touching the body can’t make sparks.

    Does a new car battery need to be registered or programmed?

    On many newer cars — BMW, Audi, VW, Mercedes, Volvo and others with a battery sensor or start-stop — yes. Without it the car keeps charging the new battery as if it were the old, worn one. In auto electrician Oleg’s experience, on an AGM battery the charging voltage then stays higher than it should, and the new battery gets overcharged. It’s done with a scan tool: “battery replacement” or “register battery”.

    Can I replace an AGM battery with a regular one?

    Not if the car came with AGM or EFB — usually cars with start-stop. Put the same type back; a regular flooded battery wears out quickly there and start-stop may stop working.

    Do I need a memory saver?

    It’s optional. Without it you may need to reset the clock, the radio presets, the one-touch windows and sometimes enter a radio code. A memory saver plugs into the OBD-II port and keeps those settings.

    Why does my car idle rough or stall after changing the battery?

    With the battery disconnected, the engine computer forgets what it learned about idle and the throttle. Let the engine idle for 5–10 minutes without touching the pedal, then drive normally; on some cars the throttle is relearned with a scan tool.

    How long does it take to change a car battery?

    Usually 15–30 minutes with a 10 mm wrench (sometimes 13 mm or a socket on an extension for the hold-down). Batteries under a seat or in the trunk take longer.

    What do I do with the old battery?

    Take it back to the store or a recycling center — don’t throw it in the trash. Most stores refund the core charge when you bring the old battery back.
