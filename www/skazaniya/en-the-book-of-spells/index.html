Lyrics

Out where Route 66 is crossing
Lived a master, old as stone;
Thick his book of spells and curses —
Read till dawn, and all alone.

Every page held signs and numbers,
Letters dark as midnight's veil:
"P — zero — four — five — seven…"
Read it right, and you'll prevail.

Late one night a girl came knocking:
"Help me, mister, if you might!
On my dash a sign is burning,
I can't see an inch tonight!"

Old man hauled the heavy volume,
Dust flew up like desert sand —
But the girl just reached her pocket:
Lit a light right in her hand!

Book of spells, oh book of curses,
Letters, numbers — dark and dread!
Read it, and the road gets clearer:
You can drive on straight ahead!

So he read the spell out plainly,
No dark crossroads deal, no price:
"Twist the gas cap till it's clicking,
Then drive on — that's my advice."

Twisted — click! — and down the highway,
Light went out in a day or so.
Old man grinned: "It's always gas caps —
And the phone knew. Now I know."

In plain words: what the tale means

The book of spells
A list of trouble codes.
P-zero-four-five-seven
Code P0457: fuel vapor leak — usually a loose gas cap.
It's always gas caps
An American mechanics' joke — and for P0457 it is often true.
