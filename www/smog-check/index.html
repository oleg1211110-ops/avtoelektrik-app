Will my car pass the smog check?

For 1996 and newer cars most states run a quick OBD-II check: the Check Engine light must be off and the readiness monitors complete. Check it yourself before you pay for the test.

How to check your car yourself

  1. Plug an inexpensive ELM327 adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard.
  2. Open the free AvtoElektrik PRO app (Android): Scanner → Readiness. With US units selected it shows whether the car is ready for the test.
  3. If a monitor isn’t ready, drive normally for a few days: a cold start, city driving and 15–20 minutes on the highway.
  4. Don’t clear codes or disconnect the battery for a few days before the test.

On iPhone the scanner works in demo mode only for now; the readiness check above works on any phone.

Questions

Which states require an emissions test?

29 states and Washington, D.C. require some form of emissions inspection (as of 2026), often only in certain counties or metro areas. For 1996 and newer cars it is usually a quick OBD-II check. Check your state DMV website for the exact rules.

What are readiness monitors?

Self-tests the engine computer runs while you drive: catalytic converter, EVAP system, oxygen sensors and others. After codes are cleared or the battery is disconnected they show “not ready” until the car has been driven enough.

How many monitors can be incomplete?

Under EPA guidance followed by most states, 2001 and newer vehicles may have one incomplete monitor and 1996–2000 vehicles two. Some states are stricter.

How do I get the monitors ready?

Drive normally for several days: include a cold start, city driving and 15–20 minutes at a steady highway speed. Don’t clear codes or disconnect the battery before the test.
