What the scanner does

App screenshots on the “Try without an adapter” virtual car.

Codes decoded

Stored, pending and permanent engine codes — with a clear name, causes and the first repair step.

Live data with normal values

RPM, temperature, voltage, fuel trims, MAF — with warm-engine normal values next to them. Graphs, big digits, recording to a table.

ABS, SRS, transmission

ABS, airbag, transmission, steering and cluster codes — Toyota/Lexus, Hyundai/Kia, VW/Skoda/Audi. Read and clear only.

Misfires by cylinder

Module tests (mode 06): see which cylinder misfires before the Check Engine light comes on.

“Were codes cleared?”

Before buying a car: distance and warm-ups since codes were cleared, readiness monitors, permanent codes.

PDF report

A ready document: codes, readiness, battery test, mileage stored in the ECU. Send it to a buyer or a customer.

Trip computer

Current and average consumption, distance, time, speed. Voice warnings about overheating, poor charging and high RPM.

Hybrid and EV battery

Charge, health (SOH), cell or block voltages: Hyundai/Kia EVs, Toyota Prius, Nissan Leaf. Read only.

Which adapter works

  • ELM327 over Bluetooth — the most common and inexpensive.
  • ELM327 Bluetooth LE (Vgate iCar Pro BLE, OBDLink CX and similar).
  • ELM327 Wi-Fi — connect the phone to the adapter’s network.
  • Scanmatik 2 PRO — via a Windows computer (AvtoElektrik PRO for Scanmatik).

Cheap ELM327 v2.1 copies often lose replies and can’t read ABS/SRS. The app has an “Adapter check” that shows what yours can do.

How to connect

  1. Download the AvtoElektrik PRO app for Android.
  2. Plug the adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard and turn the ignition on.
  3. Open “Scanner”, choose the adapter type and tap “Connect”.
  4. Check codes and live data and save a PDF report.

Questions

Is the scanner free?

Yes. The app and all scanner features are free. You only need an ELM327 adapter.

Does it work on iPhone?

Connecting to an adapter works only in the Android app. On iPhone and in a browser you can try the scanner on a virtual car.

Which cars are supported?

Petrol and diesel cars with an OBD-II port (roughly from 2001–2008). ABS, SRS and transmission — Toyota/Lexus, Hyundai/Kia, VW/Skoda/Audi on a CAN bus. J1939 trucks — in trial mode.

Can the scanner damage a module?

No: the app only reads data and clears codes with standard commands. No flashing, no settings, no writing to modules.

Can I try it without an adapter?

Yes: the Scanner section has a virtual car — healthy and with faults: lean mixture, misfires, overheating, an ABS fault, an EV and more.
