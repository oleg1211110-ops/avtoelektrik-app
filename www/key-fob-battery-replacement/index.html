Key fob battery replacement: which battery and how to change it

Most key fobs run on a 3 V coin battery, very often a CR2032. Read the code on the old one, swap it the same way up — and if the fob still doesn’t work, check the contacts. With push-button start you can still start the car with a dead fob.

2. How to change it yourself

  1. Signs the fob battery is low: it works only close to the car or after several presses, its light is dim, or the dash says “Key fob battery low” or “Key not detected”.
  2. Find the battery type: open the fob and read the code on the old battery (for example CR2032), or look in the owner’s manual. Buy exactly the same code.
  3. Open the fob: on many, slide the release and pull out the metal key first, then twist a coin or a small flat screwdriver wrapped in tape in the slot at the seam. Note which way the + side faces before you take the old battery out.
  4. Put the new battery in the same way up, without touching the circuit board with your fingers and without bending the contacts. Snap the halves together and try lock and unlock.
  5. Still doesn’t work — open it again: the battery upside down or a bent contact tab are the usual reasons. Most cars don’t need the fob reprogrammed after a battery change; if yours does, the owner’s manual describes the re-sync.
  6. Push-button start and the fob is dead: hold the fob right against the start button (or put it in the backup slot if your car has one) and press the brake and the button — the car reads the chip in the fob even with a dead battery.
  7. The spare fob doesn’t work either and the interior light is dim — it’s the car’s battery, not the fob: jump start or test the battery.
Keep coin batteries away from childrenA swallowed lithium coin battery can cause serious internal burns within hours. Store spares out of reach; if a child may have swallowed one, get emergency medical help right away.

    Questions

    What battery does my key fob take?

    Open the fob and read the code on the old battery — most use a 3 V lithium coin cell, very often CR2032, sometimes CR2025, CR2016, CR1632 or CR1620. The owner’s manual lists it too. Buy exactly the same code.

    Can I use a CR2025 instead of a CR2032?

    Better not. Both are 3 V and 20 mm wide, but a CR2025 is 2.5 mm thick and a CR2032 is 3.2 mm — a thinner battery can lose contact, a thicker one may not let the fob close. Use the same code.

    How do I know my key fob battery is low?

    The fob works only close to the car or after several presses, its light is dim or doesn’t blink, or the dash shows “Key fob battery low” or “Key not detected”.

    Do I need to reprogram my key fob after changing the battery?

    Most cars don’t. If the fob still doesn’t work, first check that the battery is the right way up and the contact touches it. Some cars need a short re-sync — the owner’s manual describes it.

    How do I start a push-button car with a dead key fob?

    Hold the fob right against the start button (or put it in the backup slot some cars have — the manual shows where) and press the brake and the button. The car reads the chip in the fob even with a dead battery.

    How long does a key fob battery last?

    Usually a few years. Keys for push-button start talk to the car all the time, so their batteries run down sooner.
