P0548 — Exhaust Gas Temperature Sensor Circuit Low (Bank 2 Sensor 1)

Don’t put off the repair

On the diagram: Speed, idle

OA fault code doesn’t yet mean the unit or sensor itself is faulty: often it’s the plug and connector leading to it.

Possible causes

  • Signal wire shorted to ground
  • Sensor connector or wiring: corrosion, an open circuit, a chafed wire
  • The sensor itself

What you can do yourself

  1. The sensor sits in the exhaust ahead of the turbo, catalyst or particulate filter. Check the connector: heat makes it corrode.
  2. The ECU may limit power to protect the turbo and the particulate filter.

Stop and don’t drive on if:

  • The Check Engine light is flashing
  • The temperature gauge is in the red or the overheating light is on
  • The red oil pressure light is on
  • Smoke from under the hood, a burning or gas smell
  • Strong jerking, knocking from the engine, or the car barely moves
OOOleg’s tip: check the connector

Often the cause is simple — there is no contact in the connector (plug). Look at the connector from the back: remove the rubber seal and you will see whether a wire is corroded or has come off completely. Corrosion shows up as green deposits. Ideally, gently pull each wire towards you one at a time: a sound wire stays in place, while a corroded one simply comes off — it needs to be repaired.

Unplug and inspect connectors with the ignition off.
