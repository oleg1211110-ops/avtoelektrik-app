The “Brake air pressure” light on the dashboard

Red lightStop drivingTrucks

Air pressure in the brake system is low — the brakes may not work properly.

What you can do yourself

  1. Don’t move off. If it comes on while driving, stop smoothly in a safe place and turn on the hazard lights.
  2. Let the engine run until the pressure builds up to normal and the light goes out. If it doesn’t build up, listen for hissing — that’s how you find the leak.
  3. If the pressure drops again or the compressor won’t build it up, don’t drive on: call a mechanic.

The description comes from the AvtoElektrik PRO app.

OOleg’s tip: the color of the symbol tells you how urgent it is

  • Red — stop and deal with the problem urgently: driving on is dangerous.
  • Yellow — needs checking to see what it is: don’t put off the diagnosis.
  • Green and blue — a system is simply working.
  • Exception — symbols of extra functions you switched on yourself: the rear fog light, ESP OFF by the button, heating, the differential lock. They can be yellow, but that’s not a fault — ignore them.

For example: a red oil pressure or overheating light — stop straight away and switch the engine off. A yellow Check Engine light with the engine running smoothly — drive on and get a diagnosis.
