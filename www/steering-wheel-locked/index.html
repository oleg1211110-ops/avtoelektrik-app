Steering wheel locked and the key won’t turn: how to unlock it

Key won’t turn in the ignition, or a push-button car shows “Steering lock”? You can almost always free the wheel yourself: with a regular key — rock the wheel and catch the moment; with push-button start — release the column and shake the wheel. Step by step, from an auto electrician.

3. What to do yourself, step by step

  1. A regular key and the wheel is locked. Gently rock the steering wheel left and right, as if catching the moment, while turning the key clockwise without force. The wheel usually locks when it was turned after the key came out, or the front wheels are pressed against a curb.
  2. Don’t force the key, especially when the steering wheel is locked: the key can snap off in the ignition.
  3. The wheel moves but the key still won’t turn — try the spare key: a worn key often can’t turn the lock cylinder. If the spare turns, have a new key cut from it.
  4. Push-button start and the dash says “Steering lock” (ESCL, ELV) — that’s the electronic steering column lock: a small motor moves its latch, and it’s part of the immobilizer. Free the wheel: release the steering column tilt/telescope lever, press Start and shake the wheel up and down — a bit harder than usual, but without jerking, so nothing breaks.
  5. Still locked — reach the steering lock under the steering column cover and, while pressing Start, tap it lightly with a small hammer: that often helps the motor pull the latch back.
  6. Check the battery: with low voltage the steering lock often doesn’t work.
  7. If it keeps happening, the steering lock and its power supply need a diagnosis: the steering column lock is part of the immobilizer, and its trouble codes show up on a scan tool.

Questions

Why won’t my key turn in the ignition?

Most often the steering wheel is locked: the lock pin is pinched by the wheel. Gently rock the steering wheel left and right, as if catching the moment, while turning the key clockwise without force. The wheel usually locks when it was turned after the key came out, or the front wheels are pressed against a curb.

How do I unlock the steering wheel on a push-button start car?

Free the wheel: release the steering column tilt/telescope lever, press Start and shake the wheel up and down — a bit harder than usual, but without jerking, so nothing breaks. Still locked — reach the steering lock under the steering column cover and, while pressing Start, tap it lightly with a small hammer: that often helps the motor pull the latch back.

What is an electronic steering column lock (ESCL)?

An electronic steering lock: a small motor pushes a latch into the steering shaft when you switch the car off and pulls it back when you start it. It’s part of the immobilizer. Check the battery: with low voltage the steering lock often doesn’t work.

The wheel moves, but the key won’t turn

The wheel moves but the key still won’t turn — try the spare key: a worn key often can’t turn the lock cylinder. If the spare turns, have a new key cut from it.

Can I force the key?

Don’t force the key, especially when the steering wheel is locked: the key can snap off in the ignition.
